CGPA Target Predictor & Semester Forecaster

Calculate the average SGPA required in remaining semesters to achieve your goal

Academic Planning Tool
Calculation Methodology:

$$\text{Target SGPA} = \frac{(\text{Desired CGPA} \times \text{Total Sems}) - (\text{Current CGPA} \times \text{Completed Sems})}{\text{Remaining Sems}}$$

* Important Note: This calculator assumes equal semester weighting. Official university degree certifications weight credits individually per course, which may introduce minor variations in final outcomes.
